Proportional Map of Alternative Energy Sources

Biomass, geothermal, and solar power plants across the U.S., with circle markers scaled to each plant's generation capacity.

Leaflet GeoJSON JavaScript CartoDB

Overview

What this map shows

This map takes a nationwide GeoJSON dataset of power plants and narrows it to three alternative energy types: biomass, geothermal, and solar. Each plant is shown as a circle sized by its megawatt generation capacity, so scale differences between facilities are visible at a glance rather than buried in an attribute table.

Approach

Data & method

The dataset required filtering by energy type before it was usable, then the generation-capacity field was mapped to marker radius so the proportional symbology actually meant something.

  • Source data filtered to Biomass, Geothermal, and Solar plant types
  • Marker radius scaled by generation capacity in megawatts
  • Dark CartoDB basemap chosen to make the colored markers stand out
