Alternative Energy Sources Across the U.S.

Mega Wat Capacity of Biomass, Geothermal, and Solar Energy Plants.

Map authored by Allyson Conway

This is a proportional symbol map where each point varies in size depending on its generating capacity in megawatts. In order to improve visual hierarchy, subtle design principles were applied such as better suited colors for each plant. Symbol sizes were scaled to reduce clutter and opacity values were adjusted so overlap of nearby plants easier to distinguish. A hover highlight was also added to each symbol maker so when the user moves their mouse over a plant the cirlce becomes slightly larger and the opacity darkens.
